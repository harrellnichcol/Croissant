Croissant

What Is a Croissant? Shape: Traditionally shaped like a crescent moon, though straight versions often indicate a pure butter recipe. Dough: Made using a process called lamination, which folds butter repeatedly between layers of yeast dough. Texture: Crispy and flaky on the outside, with soft, airy honeycombed layers inside.


History and Origins The Kipferl: The pastry started in Austria as a denser, bread-like crescent roll called the kipferl, dating back to the 13th century. Arrival in France: Austrian entrepreneur August Zang opened a Parisian bakery in the 1830s introducing steam ovens and Viennese baking styles.


French Evolution: French bakers adapted the recipe using laminated yeast dough, turning it into the modern icon by the early 20th century.


Popular Types Plain Butter Croissant: Rich and classic, made with layers of real butter.Pain au Chocolat: Rectangular pastry filled with dark chocolate. Almond Croissant: Filled and topped with sweet almond paste or marzipan. Savory Variations: Stuffed with cheese, ham, or spinach.
